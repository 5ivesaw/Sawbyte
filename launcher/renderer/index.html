1. Three Launch Modes (Auto-Downloaded)
Zero Bloat

The launcher itself is only ~1.5 MB and never comes bloated with bundled game binaries:
• Headless Mode (native): Downloads sawbyte-play (~4 MB) on first launch and connects to any Minecraft 26.3 server in ~8.2 MB of RAM with no window.
• Potato Mode (visualizer): Uses pure-Rust software 3D raymarching & 360° radar (~10.4 MB RAM, zero GPU/JVM).
• Graphical Mode (bridge): Downloads Fabric 26.3, places sawbyte-bridge-0.3.4.jar into mods/, and launches the full graphical game.

2. Visual Controls + Companion Terminal
Dual Control

• Graphical Control: Watch the live output stream on the Home tab and click visual action buttons (Walk, Jump, Raycast Mine, 3D A* Pathfind, Inspect Chest GUI) without touching a terminal.
• Companion Terminal (sawbyte>): Whenever you launch Sawbyte Launcher (or click >_ Pop Out Terminal), an interactive terminal opens alongside the GUI for power users!

3. AI Agents (MCP) & Neural Networks
RL & Agents

• AI Agents (MCP): Toggle MCP Server: ON and paste the config into Claude, Gemini, Cursor, or Antigravity to let AI play survival autonomously.
• Neural Networks (Gymnasium): Point PyTorch or SawbyteEnv(connect_addr="127.0.0.1:25580") at the live TCP port for zero-graphics sub-voxel observations at 20 TPS.

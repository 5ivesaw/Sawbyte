AI Bot Integration

Hook Up Claude, Gemini, or Custom Scripts

Sawbyte includes a built-in MCP server and SKILL.md guide so AI agents can join a Minecraft 26.3 world and play survival mode on their own.

Built-in Bot Tools
  • sawbyte_inspect_status : Read health, hunger & coordinates
  • sawbyte_milestone_save : Save milestones & death logs
  • sawbyte_navigate_astar : Walk to XYZ coordinates with 3D A*
  • sawbyte_raycast_block : Check blocks in line of sight
  • sawbyte_inventory_find : Search all 45 inventory slots
  • sawbyte_craft_recipe : Craft 2x2 and 3x3 items
Survival Progression Guide
  • Step 1: Punch trees, craft wood & stone tools
  • Step 2: Smelt iron, mine down to deepslate diamonds
  • Step 3: Build Nether portal, get blaze rods & ender pearls
  • Step 4: Find Stronghold & defeat the Ender Dragon
  • Fair Play: No creative mode or teleport cheats
